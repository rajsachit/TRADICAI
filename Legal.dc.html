Risk Disclosure

Investing in securities and derivatives involves substantial risk, including the possible loss of all capital invested. Algorithmic and leveraged strategies can lose money rapidly. Only invest capital you can afford to lose.

3.1 Market risk

The value of investments may fall as well as rise due to changes in economic conditions, interest rates, currency rates, political events, and investor sentiment. Diversification does not guarantee a profit or protect against loss.

3.2 Model and algorithmic risk

Our strategies rely on quantitative models, machine learning and statistical relationships derived from historical data. These relationships may break down, models may contain errors, and data inputs may be incomplete or incorrect. Model behaviour in unprecedented market conditions cannot be predicted.

3.3 Technology and execution risk

Automated trading depends on software, hardware, networks, exchange connectivity and third-party data feeds. Outages, latency, bugs, cyber-attacks or exchange malfunctions may cause orders to be delayed, mis-executed or not executed, leading to losses.

3.4 Leverage and derivatives

Futures and options carry leverage, so small price moves can produce large gains or losses that may exceed the initial margin. Positions may be liquidated without notice if margin requirements are not met.

3.5 Liquidity risk

In stressed markets it may be difficult or impossible to close positions at desired prices. Redemption rights may be subject to notice periods, gates, lock-ups or suspension as set out in the offering documents.

3.6 Counterparty and custody risk

We rely on brokers, clearing members and custodians. Their insolvency or operational failure could result in delays or losses.

3.7 Regulatory and tax risk

Changes in laws, regulations, margin rules, transaction taxes or tax treatment may adversely affect strategy performance or the after-tax return to investors.

3.8 Concentration and key-person risk

Strategies may at times be concentrated in particular instruments, sectors or factors. The performance of the Services depends on a small number of key personnel and proprietary systems.
